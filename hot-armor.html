How House of Thule armor works

Every visible armor piece in House of Thule is combined in a Shroud of Dreams. Unlike Underfoot, this armor does upgrade: a tier 1 piece plus a tier 2 remnant becomes the tier 2 piece, and so on up to tier 4. If you don't have the piece below, any tier can be built fresh from a remnant, a coalescing agent, and that tier's template. Group and raid armor are made exactly the same way — only the remnant changes.

Where to get the parts: Zande Vorich in The Feerrott, near the PoK book, sells the Shroud of Dreams, all eight coalescing agents, and the Inchoate (tier 2) templates. Ginale Vaultseeker, outside the zone-in to the House of Thule, sells the Nebulous (tier 3) and Amorphous (tier 4) templates. The Shroud is a three-slot container you carry, so the combine can happen anywhere.

Tiers, zones, and remnants

Remnants are named (Tier) Remnant of (Slot) — an Abstruse Remnant of Desire, for example, is a tier 1 group chest remnant. The tier word tells you the tier and whether it's group or raid; the slot word tells you the piece. They drop at random off named mobs throughout the expansion.

Every tier has its own raid remnant — nothing is shared across tiers the way Palladium was in Underfoot. For scale, tier 1 sits at about Underfoot's tier 8, and tier 2 is a small step above Underfoot's tier 9.

Coalescing agents

Bought from Zande Vorich in The Feerrott. The eight names and class pairs match Underfoot's bonding agents, but these are a separate item — Coalescing, not Bonding. One agent covers two classes, and the agent is what keys the piece to your class. You only need one when building a piece fresh; upgrades skip it.

Templates

Each tier has its own template. Tier 1 uses the player-made templates; tiers 2 through 4 come from vendors in The Feerrott. A template is only needed when building a piece fresh — if you already have the piece from the tier below, the remnant alone upgrades it.

Set names by class

The set name stays fixed per class; only the tier word changes. Group pieces read (Tier) (Set) Armor and raid pieces read (Tier) Armor of (Title), so a Warrior farms Abstruse Ironhand Armor in groups and Enigmatic Armor of the Protector on raids.

Odds and ends

Wrist focus swaps

Shadow Knights, Shamans, Necromancers and Beastlords can change a finished wrist piece's focus effect by placing it alone in a Shroud of Dreams — no Box of Latent Energies needed this time. Worth knowing before anyone rolls on a second Remnant of Truth.

Terror Infused armor

Terror Infused armor is standalone: it can't be upgraded and can't be used in any combine. The set covers head, arms, chest, legs and feet for every class at roughly 919–936 HP/Mana — just under tier 1 group armor, which makes it useful filler while remnant luck sorts itself out.

Dream Motes

Rotharran Radhelm, the adventure merchant, sells jewelry and accessories for Dream Motes — finger, ear, waist, face, back, neck, shoulder, charm and range. Some items only open up as faction climbs through apprehensive, indifferent, amiable and kindly.

Getting there

Clicking the Oggok portal in the Plane of Knowledge now asks whether you want The Feerrott or Feerrott, the Dream. The Dream is the base zone of House of Thule.
