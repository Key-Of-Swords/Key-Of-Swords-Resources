How Underfoot armor works

Every visible armor piece in Underfoot is built from three parts combined in a kiln. The combine does not fail. There is no upgrade chain — tier 8 armor does not consume tier 7 armor, and raid armor is not progressive either. Each piece stands on its own, so a character can skip straight to whatever tier they can actually farm.

Where to do it: the Useful Automated Vendor sits in Brell's Rest, across from the Gatekeeper up the ramp toward the Shining City. There is a kiln next to it, so agent purchase and combine happen in the same spot.

Tiers, zones, and clay

Clay is named (Material) Encrusted (Slot) Clay. The material tells you the tier; the slot word tells you the piece. Each named mob has one slot it drops most often, with a smaller chance at the others.

Templates

Templates are tradeable and player-made, so a guild crafter can supply the whole raid. Which template you need depends on your armor class, not your tier — the same template works at every tier.

Template combines

  • Vanadium Plate / Chain — Brimstone Coal, Metal Tempering Chemicals, the matching mold, Smithy Hammer, and 1–3 Vanadium Ore depending on slot.
  • Immaculate Silk — Faysilk Filament, Silk Curing Chemicals, Silk Pattern, Simple Sewing Needle, and 2–6 Immaculate Silk depending on slot.
  • Immaculate Leather — Faysilk Filament, Tanning Chemicals, Leather Pattern, Simple Sewing Needle, and 1–3 Immaculate Animal Pelt depending on slot.

The set name stays fixed per class; only the material word changes with tier. Group and raid lines use different names entirely, so a Warrior farms Earthwall in groups and Terraguard on raids.

Odds and ends

Wrist focus swaps

Shadow Knights, Shamans, Necromancers and Beastlords can put a finished wrist piece into a Box of Latent Energies — also sold by the Useful Automated Vendor — to change its focus effect. Worth knowing before anyone rolls on a second Carpal clay.

All/All drops in Volska's Husk

Four non-clay pieces drop at random in Volska's Husk and fit any class, which makes them useful filler while clay luck sorts itself out: Autarchian Sandals (feet), Autarchian Fistwraps (hands), Autarchian Linked Leggings (legs), and the Autarchian Robe (chest).

Access reminders

Tier 8 opens after clearing one group mission in each of the six tier 6 and 7 zones — the Gatekeeper in Brell's Rest tracks it and will name the next zone if you hail him with "enter." Tier 9 opens after the four tier 8 missions, and the Convorteum needs the Greken hatchlings mission out of Volska's Husk.
