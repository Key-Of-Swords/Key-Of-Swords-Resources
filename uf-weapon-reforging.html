How reforging works

Raid loot in Underfoot rarely lands on the person who wants it. The Weapon Reforging tasks fix that: hand two unwanted raid weapons to a smith in Kernagir, and she remakes them into one weapon of your choosing from the same raid tier. She keeps the leftover ore as her fee. It is a straight two-for-one swap — nothing to farm, no tradeskill, no platinum.

Crushing is permanent. Once Saria breaks a weapon down it cannot be restored. Check the reward list for your tier before you hand anything over, and make sure the weapon you want is actually on it.

Saria Everhot

All three tasks come from the same NPC. Saria Everhot stands in the forge in Kernagir, the Shining City, at roughly 1160, 625, 320. The forge is easy to overshoot — use the Find window (Ctrl+F) to path straight to her.

Hailing her opens a short conversation about her family's "amazing skills," and at the end a task window offers all three reforging tasks at once — one per raid tier. Pick the tier that matches the weapons you're turning in.

Tiers and crushed metals

The three tasks follow the same raid tiers as Underfoot raid armor, and use the same material names. A tier 6 or 7 raid weapon crushes to Palladium, tier 8 to Iridium, tier 9 to Rhodium. You can only swap within a tier — two Palladium weapons can never become a Rhodium one.

Rewards are gated by your kills. Saria only offers weapons from raid events you personally have completed. If you've never beaten an event, its weapons won't show up in your reward window — so a character who skipped a raid can't use this to shop for that raid's loot.

Walkthrough

  1. Collect two unwanted raid weapons from the same tier.
  2. Hail Saria Everhot in the Kernagir forge and click through the dialogue: yes → skills → another → price → weapon → crush → remake → sure.
  3. Accept the matching task from the window — Palladium, Iridium, or Rhodium.
  4. Hand her the first weapon. She returns one Crushed (metal).
  5. Hand her the second weapon. She returns a second Crushed (metal).
  6. Hand both crushed metals back. The task completes and a reward window opens.
  7. Pick your weapon from the list of events you've beaten.
Not ready to choose? The Rhodium reward list includes 2x Crushed Rhodium as an option, which hands your metal back so you can finish the task later — useful if the weapon you want is from an event you haven't killed yet.

Reward lists

Palladium — tiers 6 & 7

Any of the 18 weapons from the tier 6 and 7 raid events you've completed. Players report the pool covering the Fippy, Beast, Masked Invaders, Unburrowing, Brath, and both Arthicrex events (the Guardians and the Queen). One reported swap: a Black Revenge and a Bloodied Crook of Wrath turned into an Edge of Oblivion.

Any weapon from the Fungal Forest, Lichen Creep, and three Brell's Temple raid events you've completed. No confirmed per-weapon list has been published for this tier yet.

Odds and ends

Why trade two for one?

Because the drop table doesn't care what your raid needs. If the guild has a pile of weapons nobody wants and the one your tank is waiting on never drops, two spares become the right one. It's also a clean way to convert DPS weapons into aggro weapons for a tank, or the reverse.

Before you crush

  • Check the list — open the task window and look at what's actually offered to you before handing over a single weapon.
  • Check lore — don't pick a lore weapon you already own.
  • Match the tier — both weapons must crush to the same metal for the task you accepted.

Access reminder

Saria lives in Kernagir, a tier 8 zone. Characters still working through tier 6 and 7 progression need the six-zone Gatekeeper flag before they can reach her, even for the Palladium task.
