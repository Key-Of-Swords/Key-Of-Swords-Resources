Overview

Fippy despawns every 20% health.
Each phase (every 20% of Fippy's health) has a timer of 10 minutes (first aggro to respawn). If this timer runs out, Fippy starts hitting for a max ~75,000 and AE rampaging and flurrying about every round — stay within the timer.
Each time Fippy despawns, a wave of adds spawns and attacks. You have two minutes to kill these adds before Fippy respawns, at which point any remaining adds will attempt to run to Fippy's position and heal him.
If you do not aggro Fippy within a certain amount of time, the event will reset.

Fippy is tethered to the northern half of the zone.
If you pull him towards the zone-in or to the valleys on either side, he'll warp back to his spawnpoint.

Strategy

KOS Raid Strategy

  1. Assign Knight to pull Fippy for ramp tank.
100–80%
  1. DPS Fippy to 80%.
  2. Casters burn your mana bar on this phase, as you will be medding next phase.
  3. At 80%, 15x "shade of Fippy Darkpaw" spawn all around the lava area. They hit for a max ~8,000 and are unmezzable, but rootable/snareable/stunnable.
  4. Follow MA and kill adds.
  5. They each drop a bane item for use in the next phase.
  6. Free grab the bane items for MELEE to grab.
80–60%
  1. Fippy Darkpaw the Undying is immune to most damage except for bane procs.
  2. Single target ramp and occasional AE Ramp for full damage.
  3. He will cast FD during this phase so be prepared to STAND.
  4. Pets, nukes, non-bane damage will be worthless, so CASTERS use your mana bars on phase 1 and med during this phase.
  5. Healers and casters move up the ramp out of FD range (200 range). Casters will med up during this phase.
  6. Drop two wards at melee to mitigate the 10k DD.
  7. Melee that got the bane weapon, you need to click the items to get buff that allows bane damage on Fippy. Once clicked the item poofs. If you die, the bane buff goes away and can't get it back.
  8. Melee remove all your weapons and hand to hand, otherwise, you will get aggro and risk dying and losing your bane buff.
  9. At 60%, 8x "shade of Fippy Darkpaw" spawn all around the lava area. They hit for a max ~8,000 and are unmezzable, but rootable/snareable/stunnable.
  10. Follow MA and kill adds.
60–40%
  1. During this phase, he alternates between a powered-up mode and his regular state. While he's powered up, he can hit for upwards of 22,500. Regular = ~18,500.
  2. Also during this phase, he warps around to random people on his hatelist, casting the spell "Shadow Strike" on them:
  3. Everyone must stack up on the phase.
  4. At 40%, he despawns. At this point, 8x "shade of Fippy Darkpaw" spawn.
  5. Follow MA and kill adds.
40–20%
  1. He casts an AE snare and a single-target charm (hits multiple people at a time):
  2. Part way through this phase, 4-5 sabertooth gnolls spawn and attack. They hit for a max ~8,000; are mezzable as well as rootable and snareable.
  3. The sabertooth adds are timed, so you'll have to deal with 2-4 waves of these during this phase, depending on your DPS against Fippy.
  4. Burn DPS disc on Fippy.
  5. At 20%, he despawns. At this point, 8x "shade of Fippy Darkpaw" spawn.
  6. Keep the sabertooth gnolls mezzed, kill the "shade of Fippy Darkpaw" when they spawn, then kill the sabertooth gnolls.
20–0%
  1. Part way through this phase, 4-5 sabertooth gnolls spawn and attack. They hit for a max ~8,000; are mezzable as well as rootable and snareable.
  2. The sabertooth adds are timed, so you'll have to deal with 2-4 waves of these during this phase, depending on your DPS against Fippy.
  3. Burn any remaining DPS disc on Fippy.
  4. Also appearing towards the end of this phase are several shades of Fippy Darkpaw. These will not aggro on the raid. Instead, they will run to Fippy's location and attempt to heal him. This seems to be avoidable by either killing the shades or moving Fippy around.
  5. Kill the shades as they spawn.
  6. Keep the sabertooth gnolls mezzed and kill them after Fippy dies.

The first emote fires when a cliknar bladeguard begins casting Barrier of Blades, a large self-damage shield lasting two ticks — swap to a non-damage main-hand weapon set now.
The second emote confirms the shield has landed and is active.

Overview

Event:
Hail, Protauris Sithirhin and run through the text ("Destroyed" then "We do hope!"). This triggers the first wave of cliknar.
All event mobs are leashed to the area they're in. You can't allow them to move too far past the two gnomes, otherwise they'll leash and the event MAY reset.
Any mobs that leash reset their health to 100%, strip their debuffs, and warp back to their spawnpoint.
You can obtain a Barrel of Unstable Potions from either gnome at any time.
If things start going badly, you can say "flee" to either of the Cavernaut gnomes to zone out.
Once ALL mobs are dead, the person with the bomb must stand next to the large hill and right-click it. An explosive barrel spawns and detonates shortly thereafter.

There are 7 waves, then the final boss. All are timed.
All waves are timed (ready or not, they'll spawn!) and are a mix of adult cliknar (named "a cliknar _____") and young cliknar ("a young _____").
You'll get 6-8 cliknar per wave.
The young ones are mezzable while the adult ones are not.
Mobs are rootable and should be rooted.

The raid needs to stack up and stay central.

Tanks:
Mobs hit hard, so don't think you're going to AE Agro and survive.

Pallies:
Make sure you use level appropriate stuns on the adults. Adult chitinguards are unstunnable (Wave 4).
The adds have a mix of AE's, including Blind, Melee/Spell slow, DoT's, and Roots. Splash of Sanctification can remove many of these afflictions.

Enchanters:
Setup a PBAE stun chain at the camp and keep the Young mezzed.

Bards:
At certain intervals through the event, mobs called "a cliknar messenger" spawn in the northeast area and walk towards Protauris Sithirhin. These mobs do not attack. These messengers can also be charmed to obtain a "Blob of Mind Control Jelly" from them which in turn can be used to charm any of the cliknar in the event.
When you see one, CHARM it, and it'll despawn, and a Blob of Mind Control Jelly will appear on your cursor. This is a one shot clicky charm ALL/ALL item (duration 1.6 mins).
Use this Jelly later to charm the mutabeetles that spawn during the Magrix stage (Wave 7). Use your new found pet against the Magrix's as assigned.

A total of five messengers spawn, giving you opportunity to obtain five charm jellies.
The charm has a duration of 1.6 minutes.
These mobs have a lot of HPs.

Healers:
During the Magrix stage, Leenax casts Arcane Disjunction, a 2k DoT Mana Drain. 6 Corruption Counters, keep it cured.

Rangers & Druids:
Use Vinelash Cascade to root the adds on incoming.

Casters:
NO AOE. "Youngs" will be mezzed through the entire event.

Overview

Grunkuck is permanently rooted at all times in the event.
Does not see invis.
Has an aura that will drop invis if you get too close.

Mobs in this zone are semi-belly casters, meaning you have to be in melee range to land.

Grunkuck occasionally tosses random people to the whelps in the surrounding water (these mobs are in groups of four and are unattackable). You'll see this emote as she does this:
Grunkuck tosses {S} to her young.
If this happens to you, swim away from the mobs quickly as they each hit for ~6,000.

She will also occasionally summon people who are outside her melee range. This places you directly under her and is unrelated to your position on her hatelist.

DO NOT STAND ON THE SIDE OF EITHER GRUNKUCK OR HER MATE.

Strategy

KOS Raid Strategy

  1. DPS Grunkuck down to 70%.
  2. 100%–70%: Grunkuck is perma-rooted.
  3. Need to obey the emotes during this phase.
70% Warp
  1. At 70% health, Grunkuck warps to the southwest coast of the lake.
  2. At this point, four dimensional beast adolescents spawn around her. They must be killed quickly, as they heal Grunkuck's health. They are perma-rooted.
  3. They cast a knockback/18k DD/stun.
  4. These mobs are STUNNABLE. Keep them stunned.
  5. Follow MA and kill them clockwise.
  6. Then continue to DPS Grunkuck, but DO NOT GO BELOW 50%.
2nd Warp
  1. A few minutes after warping to the southwest coast, Grunkuck warps again — this time to the northwest coast.
  2. Another four adolescents spawn. Kill them and continue attacking Grunkuck down to 43%.
43%
  1. Once Grunkuck reaches 43% health, "Kelmuck, Grunkuck's mate" spawns close by and slithers up to Grunkuck's location.
  2. Face the two nameds NORTH and SOUTH, one in each direction.
  3. Burn down Grunkuck.
  4. Then burn down the Mate.
  5. DO NOT STAND ON THE SIDE OF EITHER MOB.
Adds
  1. After the mate spawns, the dimensional beast whelps in the water go aggro (one group of four at a time) and charge the raid. They each hit for a max ~6,000.
  2. In addition to those, several mobs called "an underdeveloped whelp" spawn and attack (max hit ~6,500). The only way to kill these is to kite them. Without anyone to attack, they'll go hungry and gradually drop to 0% on their own.
  3. Kite group needs to kite ALL adds.
  4. Any remaining adds despawn upon the deaths of Grunkuck and Kelmuck.
